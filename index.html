About SkyWork

Where you work matters. We built ours fifteen floors up.

SkyWork Borivali is a premium yet accessible workspace for Mumbai's professionals, steps from Borivali station, with panoramic city views.

Two identical setups on the same floor with twenty-six seats in all: eighteen dedicated desks across the open collaborative areas, and two four-seat private cabins for focused work and client calls.

We care about your health as much as your productivity: purified air and water, a microwave and kettle for homemade meals, and deliberately no pantry. Step out, take a real break, come back sharper.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our workspace and facilities

We've intentionally chosen not to have a traditional pantry. Here's why: we believe in promoting your health and well-being. Long work hours require regular movement and fresh air. By encouraging you to step outside for tea or coffee at nearby cafes (we're steps from Borivali station with plenty of options), you get to stretch, socialize with colleagues, refresh your mind, and return more focused. We do provide a microwave for heating your homemade vegetarian meals, along with water purifiers for safe hydration.

We have a microwave available for members to heat their vegetarian food. We encourage bringing healthy, homemade meals. Water purifiers are installed throughout the space for safe drinking water. While we don't provide tea/coffee on-site, our prime location means cafes and restaurants are just steps away.

Yes! We encourage bringing your own healthy vegetarian meals. Our microwave is available for heating food. Please note: We strictly maintain a vegetarian-only policy at our workspace. This helps us maintain a clean, respectful environment for all members. We believe homemade, nutritious vegetarian meals are better for your health and productivity.

Your health and safety are our priorities. We have air purifiers for clean air quality, water purifiers for safe hydration, 24/7 security cameras for safety, and ergonomic seating for your comfort. Our space is cleaned regularly, and we maintain a professional, healthy work environment.

We're open Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 9 PM. We're closed on Sundays to ensure everyone gets proper rest and work-life balance.

We're located in a residential building steps from Borivali railway station, making public transport the most convenient option. We offer two-wheeler (bike/scooter) parking for our members. Four-wheeler parking is not available due to the residential nature of our building. Our prime station location eliminates the need for car parking for most members who commute by train.
